Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

BPM is a minimalist heart rate display app. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This policy explains what data we handle and why.

Summary

  • BPM does not require an account. Optional voice notes may contain personal information you choose to record.
  • Measured heart rate data stays on your device unless you explicitly choose to share it.
  • With your permission, AI voice capture sends your recording to OpenAI to create editable workout notes. This content is not used to train OpenAI models.
  • If you enable sharing, your heart rate is sent to your backend and retained briefly so others with your code can view it.
  • We collect limited, non-personal app usage and device information through TelemetryDeck to understand app reliability and usage.

Data We Handle

Heart Rate (BPM), Max, and Average (last hour)

  • Source: Your Bluetooth LE heart rate strap via CoreBluetooth.
  • On-device use: Display and simple stats.
  • Optional sharing: When you enable sharing, BPM/max/avg and a timestamp are sent to your backend service and associated only with a random 6-digit code and session token.

Sharing and Retention

  • When sharing is ON:
    • Data is transmitted over HTTPS to your backend (default deployment: Vercel + KV/Upstash).
    • Share sessions expire automatically after 90 minutes.
  • When sharing is OFF:
    • BPM does not transmit measured heart rate samples through live sharing.

Optional AI Voice Capture

  • BPM records up to 60 seconds on your device. Before the first upload on each device, BPM explains the processing and asks you to choose “Send recording to OpenAI” or “Cancel.” After permission is granted, only recordings you explicitly send go through the BPM backend to OpenAI.
  • OpenAI transcribes the recording and creates a title and detailed description. Review and edit the result before saving.
  • Only the recording and its transcript are used for this request. BPM does not attach measured heart rate samples, HealthKit records, existing notes, or workout history.
  • BPM does not persist recordings, transcripts, or generated text on its backend or include them in application logs. Temporary device recordings are removed after successful generation, when discarded, or when the capture form closes. Failed requests retain the recording while the form remains open so you can retry.
  • Your recording and transcript may contain fitness or health information you choose to mention, such as exercises, weights, effort, or discomfort. They may also contain identifying information if you say it. BPM does not anonymize the words you record.
  • Model-improvement sharing is disabled for the OpenAI project used by BPM. OpenAI does not use these requests to train or improve its models. Under OpenAI's current API data controls, the transcription endpoint does not retain audio as application state or in abuse-monitoring logs. The text-generation endpoint may retain transcripts and generated notes in abuse-monitoring logs for up to 30 days, or longer when required by law or necessary for safety. BPM disables stored response state; that does not disable these safety logs.
  • The backend temporarily stores daily request counts and hashed IP-derived identifiers to limit abuse. These counters expire after approximately 25 hours. Hosting providers may retain operational request metadata.
  • You can always enter the title and description manually without using AI voice capture.

Analytics

  • TelemetryDeck receives app launch and session activity, app version, device type, operating system, and a non-personal device identifier for analytics.
  • Analytics never include heart rate data, workout details, share codes, or other health information.
  • Analytics data is not linked to your identity and is not used for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Identifiers and Tracking

  • We do not use advertising identifiers or track users across apps or websites.
  • The share code and token are random and not linked to your identity.
  • Standard host logs (for example IP address) may be recorded by your hosting provider for operations/security.

Device Permissions

  • Bluetooth: Used to discover and connect to your heart rate strap.
  • Microphone: Used only when you record workout voice notes.

Security

  • Data in transit uses HTTPS.
  • The backend stores minimal, ephemeral data needed for sharing functionality.

Your Choices

  • Keep heart-rate sharing off and enter workout notes manually if you do not want workout content transmitted.
  • You can stop sharing at any time in the app.
  • In Settings → AI Voice Privacy, choose “Reset AI voice permission” to withdraw the saved permission. BPM will ask again before any further voice upload. This permission is stored on your device and is not synced through iCloud.
  • You can deny or revoke microphone access in iOS Settings. Recording and AI processing are optional; manual workout entry remains available.
  • Closing the capture form deletes its temporary recording. Deleting a saved workout removes it from BPM's workout history. Resetting permission or deleting a workout does not erase content already processed by OpenAI or subject to its retention rules. For privacy or deletion requests, contact us at vibecodeinc@proton.me; we will explain what data we hold and what we can identify or delete.
