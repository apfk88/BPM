Chest straps are the gold standard for a reason.

I built BPM because my wrist monitors kept giving me bogus readings, especially during workouts with a lot of arm movement like kettlebell swings and snatches. I wanted readings from a chest strap, on a screen I could actually see. Nothing else.

Chest straps measure your heart’s electrical activity. Wrist monitors use light, which can be thrown off by gripping, wrist flexion, and movement. For lifting and intervals, a good chest strap is the better tool.

Contact us ↗
Which heart rate monitors work?

BPM works with standard Bluetooth Low Energy heart rate monitors. Tested with Polar H10 and Morpheus chest straps.

For HRV, your monitor also needs to transmit RR intervals.

Do I need a separate heart rate monitor?

Yes. BPM displays readings from a compatible Bluetooth monitor. Your iPhone doesn’t measure your pulse on its own.

Is live sharing free?

Yes. Share a code to let someone follow your live heart rate. Sessions expire after 90 minutes.

What does the HRV test measure?

BPM calculates RMSSD from your monitor’s RR intervals over two minutes. Results are calculated on your device and saved to your history.

How are calories calculated?

From your heart rate and profile, with resting heart rate and VO₂ max when available. Active calories exclude resting energy use. These are estimates.

Where does my data go?

Heart rate data stays on your device unless you choose to share or sync it. Optional voice notes are processed by OpenAI. BPM uses limited usage analytics.
